Chéri is for tending friendships through things. What someone mentioned they
love. The idea you had for them in a shop, saved in two taps. Which of your
books is at whose house, and which of theirs is here. A birthday in three
weeks, with time to act.

It keeps the texture of a friendship without ever making it feel like a ledger.
