Loop is for the part of the day that is yours. Meditation, prayer, an hour of
deep work, a long stretch, sleep. You set a length, pick a sound or none, and
begin.

There is no catalogue, no teacher, no course. If you have a recording you love,
bring it. If you use Apple Music, play from there. Bells can mark the start,
the intervals and the end, and on an Apple Watch you feel them on your wrist.

It does one thing and then gets out of the way. The hope is that one day you
will not need it at all.
