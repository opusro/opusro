Loop 1.0.2 is in the App Store. It is the first update since launch and most
of it is under the surface: the connection between iPhone and Apple Watch was
rebuilt from the ground up, and the things you touch most often were retuned.

## What is new

**Bells on the wrist.** With an Apple Watch, the start of a session, each
interval and the end arrive as a tap on the wrist as well as a sound. There is
also a Vibration Only bell for when a sound is not welcome.

**Sessions that start on the Watch.** You can begin a session on the Watch
while the iPhone is somewhere else. It syncs back when the two meet again.

**A new timer wheel.** Rebuilt on the phone's own scrolling with Core Haptics,
and a rolling odometer face. [We wrote about how it feels](/notes/2026/how-the-wheel-feels/).

**Cover Flow for your soundtracks.** Hold to lift and reorder, tap to explore
the built-in cover variants.

**Small things.** Mute from the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island. Swipe the
wheel sideways to change presets. Keep the screen awake during a session. Edit
the current preset from the Watch.

## What changed

One bell per preset, with a new set of bell sounds. The procedural sounds were
retuned: warmer noise, retuned binaural beats, and everything loudness matched
so switching between them does not surprise you. The icon set was refreshed and
the settings screens flattened. Profile metrics now default to All Time.

## What was fixed

The paywall price would not load for some people. Fixed, and the way the app
decides whether a purchase exists is now one deterministic check, so people
who bought the original paid version are no longer locked out on a new device.

The Dynamic Island no longer keeps counting after another app interrupts your
audio, or after a force quit, and its controls work after a cold launch.

Guards against iCloud data loss across sessions, presets and soundtracks. Many
Watch sync fixes. Per-preset reminders that are genuinely per preset.

If something is still not behaving, write to hello@opus.ro.
